# YEM'S: case study inventory

Factual inventory of what went into the Yem's website, compiled on 2026-09-27
from the repository `abou-arch/Site_ecommerce_Yem-s` at commit `b84a317`
(branch `claude/vibrant-galileo-2762ai`, which contains all of `main`).

Conventions:

- Every statement points to a file, a commit hash or a command result.
- **(inferred)** marks an interpretation that the evidence suggests but does not prove.
- Anything that cannot be determined from the repository is listed in
  section 7, "Unknown — ask Abou".
- Quotes from the site are in French, with a short English gloss where useful.

## 1. Project snapshot

### What the site is

- **Brand:** Yem's, slogan « Différents par les détails » ("Different by the
  details"). Source: `data/products.json` → `site.brand`, `site.slogan`.
- **Business:** a shoe and leather-goods house in Cotonou with its own atelier.
  It sells two lines (`data/products.json` → `site.origines`):
  - **l'atelier**: « Fabriqué à Cotonou », hand-sewn, with lifetime resoling;
  - **la sélection**: « Choisi par l'atelier », sourced elsewhere, inspected,
    numbered and exchangeable for one year.
- **Markets:** Cotonou (Benin) and Abidjan (Côte d'Ivoire) (`site.cities`).
  Checkout also accepts Togo, Senegal and Niger, with shipping fees of 0 F
  (BJ, CI), 2,000 F (TG) and 3,000 F (SN, NE). Sources:
  `api/_lib/http.js` (`COUNTRIES`), `api/_lib/catalog.js` (`SHIPPING`).
- **Target customer**, as written in the brand charter: « Un homme de 28 à
  50 ans, à Cotonou ou Abidjan, qui a un travail où l'on regarde comment il est
  habillé » ("a man aged 28–50 whose job means people look at how he dresses").
  Source: `marque/charte-de-marque.md`, section « À qui l'on parle ».
- **Language:** French only (`<html lang="fr">`, `og:locale fr_FR`).
- **Domain:** `maisonyems.com` (`site.url`). According to `wrangler.toml` the
  domain is bought, but its routes are commented out until it shows as
  "Active" in Cloudflare.

### Pages

23 pages are generated by `tools/build.py`: 15 at the root and 8 under
`produit/`. `marque/planche-identite.html` is a hand-made brand board on top of
those.

| **La sélection** | `selection.html` | Curated line, 8 products |
| **L'atelier** | `a-venir.html` | « Ce qui se prépare »: announcement page for the atelier line, which has not been photographed yet |
| **Le sur-mesure** / « Composer ma paire » | `configurateur.html` | Bespoke configurator |
| Product pages | `produit/*.html` (8) | Gallery per colour, sizes, accordions, related products |
| Panier / Commande / Confirmation | `panier.html`, `checkout.html`, `commande-confirmee.html` | Ordering flow |

« Le sur-mesure » and « Service client ». A « Composer ma paire » button and the
cart sit in the header of every page (`data/products.json` → `site.nav`,
`templates/partials/header.html`).

### Main features

- **JSON catalogue:** 16 products in `data/products.json`. The 8 « sélection »
  shoes (45,000–60,000 F) are orderable. The other 8 are flagged `a_venir`
  (not yet orderable): 4 atelier shoe shapes, a belt, a wallet, a cream and
  shoe trees.
- **Product pages:** a gallery per colour, a size picker, named colour buttons,
  accordions (construction, care, delivery and returns), related products,
  and an availability badge (green « Au Bénin », amber « En route », red
  « Indisponible »). Source: `tools/build.py` (`build_product`, `badge`).
- **Bespoke configurator:** 6 steps: shape (4), leather (4), thread
  (informational), sole (2), size (39–46) and initials. The price updates
  live and is recomputed on the server; a 50% deposit applies. Sources:
  `configurateur.html`, `assets/js/configurator.js`, `site.bespoke`.
- **Cart and checkout:** the cart lives in `localStorage`. Checkout takes no
  payment. The server recalculates every amount and issues an order reference
  `YMS-DDMM-NNNN`. Sources: `assets/js/main.js`, `assets/js/checkout.js`,
  `api/_lib/handlers.js`, `api/_lib/db.js`.
- **Atelier notification:** through the WhatsApp Cloud API when configured,
  otherwise a pre-filled `wa.me` link in the admin; e-mail through Cloudflare
  Email Sending (not enabled yet). Sources: `api/_lib/whatsapp.js`,
  `api/_lib/courriel.js`.
- **Online payment (KkiaPay):** fully implemented (widget, server-side
  verification, webhook) but switched off with `PAYMENT_MODE = "offline"`.
  Sources: `api/_lib/kkiapay.js`, `wrangler.toml`.
- **Admin:**
  - orders: status filters, a status workflow, a WhatsApp link and bulk
    anonymisation;
  - catalogue: edit price, availability and short text, hide a piece, upload
    photos to R2.
  
  Catalogue edits are applied to the static pages at the edge without a
  rebuild. Sources: `admin.html`, `assets/js/admin.js`,
  `api/_lib/overrides.js`, `api/_lib/rewrite.js`, `api/_lib/media.js`.
- **Disclosure of AI visuals:** a footer note on every page, a section in the
  legal notice, and a « Visuel IA » label (only on some pages, see §6).
  Sources: `templates/partials/footer.html`,
  `templates/pages/mentions-legales.html`.

## 2. Tasks performed

Grouped by discipline. Each line gives what was done, then the proof.

### Discovery and structure

- **Information architecture.** Four nav entries: three say what you can buy,
  the fourth what happens if something goes wrong. There is no "Home" entry.
  Proof: comment `site._nav` in `data/products.json`.
- **Two-line product model** (atelier and sélection), each product tagged with
  `origine`. Proof: `site.origines`, `products[].origine`.
- **Pieces that cannot be shown yet are hidden, not faked.** The `a_venir`
  flag removes them from grids, product pages and the cart. Proof:
  `site._a_venir`, `tools/build.py` (`Builder.__init__`), commit `44cad50`.
- **Client deliverables checklist:** prices, photos, WhatsApp number, legal
  information, prioritised in three blocks. Proof:
  `docs/a-fournir-par-le-client.md` (commit `4d8e4fc`).
- **Hand-over docs:** architecture in `docs/back-end.md`, deployment guide in
  `docs/mise-en-ligne-cloudflare.md`, how to add products in `README.md`.

### Copywriting

  Proof: `templates/pages/index.html`, `README.md` (« Copy »).

  - External: the sole that gives way on a Saturday.
  - Internal: the gaze dropping to your feet.

### Features

- **Configurator.** The price is recomputed from data on every change, never
  accumulated. The button stays disabled until shape, leather, sole and size
  are chosen. Proof: `assets/js/configurator.js`, `README.md`
  (« Le configurateur »).
- **Ordering without upfront payment:**
  - « À la livraison » (on delivery) or « Par transfert Mobile Money »;
  - a bespoke item forces transfer with a 50% deposit;
  - the confirmation page shows the reference and a message that depends on
    the payment mode.
  
  Proof: `checkout.html` (generated by `build_checkout`),
  `api/_lib/handlers.js` (`createOrder`), `commande-confirmee.html`.
- **Server-side pricing:** « Le panier dit ce que le client achète. Jamais
  combien ça coûte » ("the cart says what the customer buys, never what it
  costs"). Proof: `api/_lib/catalog.js`, `docs/back-end.md`.
- **Atelier admin.**
  - Orders: status filters and a status workflow with nine statuses from
    `pending` to `refunded`, where each status offers only sensible next
    steps. There is also a WhatsApp link and bulk anonymisation by date.
  - Catalogue: live overrides of price, availability and short text, a
    hide/show switch, photo upload with dimensions read from the file bytes,
    and a change log.
  
  Proof: `assets/js/admin.js`, `api/_lib/handlers.js`,
  `db/migration-002-catalogue-editable.sql`, commits `c5f6dde` and `defd7ca`.

### Integrations

- **WhatsApp:**
  - `wa.me` links in the footer of every page, plus the final CTA, the
    service client page, « L'atelier », the order confirmation and the legal
    pages;
  - order notification to the owner through the WhatsApp Cloud API, falling
    back to a pre-filled `wa.me` link in the admin.
  
  Proof: `api/_lib/whatsapp.js`.
- **Mobile Money and cards through KkiaPay:**
  - the widget opens with the amount returned by the server;
  - the transaction is verified on the server with the private key;
  - the webhook is authenticated by a secret header;
  - confirmation is idempotent through a unique `transaction_id`.
  
  All of this is disabled until a merchant account exists. Proof:
  `api/_lib/kkiapay.js`, `db/schema.sql`, `docs/back-end.md`.
- **E-mail order alert** through Cloudflare Email Sending to a verified
  address. It is implemented but the binding is commented out. Proof:
  `api/_lib/courriel.js`, `wrangler.toml`, commit `affc597`.
- **PostgreSQL through Hyperdrive.**
  - Orders are stored as a snapshot: prices and names are copied into each
    order line.
  - Amounts are integers in FCFA.
  - Constraints enforce `total = subtotal + shipping` and
    `line_total = unit_price × qty`.
  
  Proof: `db/schema.sql`.
- **Cloudflare R2** for atelier photos (disabled). Proof: `api/_lib/media.js`,
  commit `29904da`.
- **No CMS.** The catalogue lives in `data/products.json`; the only
  editable-at-runtime fields are the admin overrides.
- **No analytics or tracker.** No such script appears anywhere, and the
  privacy page says none is installed. Proof: search of all `.html` and `.js`
  files; `templates/pages/donnees-personnelles.html`.

### SEO, performance and accessibility

- **SEO:**
  - a unique title and description per page, canonical URLs;
  - Open Graph tags (absolute `og:image` on product pages);
  - JSON-LD: `Organization` on the home page, `CollectionPage` on categories,
    `Product` with an `Offer` in XOF;
  - `sitemap.xml` generated from the pages actually written (18 URLs), which
    leaves out the ordering flow;
  - `robots.txt` with the sitemap and disallow rules.
  
  Proof: `tools/build.py` (`page`, `json_ld`, `build_sitemap`), `sitemap.xml`,
  `robots.txt`.
- **Images:**
  - WebP plus JPEG at 500 and 1000 px wide, with `srcset`/`sizes`;
  - explicit `width`/`height` to avoid layout shift;
  - `loading="lazy"` and `decoding="async"`.
  
  A comment in `tools/images_ia.py` states that raw PNGs had pushed the home
  page from 0.7 MB to 7.6 MB, and that the script is the fix. Proof:
  `tools/images_ia.py`, `tools/build.py` (`picture`), commit `18e58a2`.
- **Other performance work:**
  - non-blocking font loading;
  - asset fingerprinting (`?v=` content hash) with immutable caching;
  - HTML set to `must-revalidate`;
  - videos with `preload="none"`.
  
  Proof: `templates/base.html`, `tools/build.py` (`version_assets`),
  `_headers`, commit `4d8e4fc`.
- **Weight measured locally.** Measured on 2026-09-27 with headless Chromium,
  a static server, no compression and Google Fonts blocked; not a production
  measurement.
  
  | Page | Mobile 390×844 | Desktop 1440×900 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Home | 10 requests, 244 KB | 11 requests, 819 KB (550 KB of video) |
  | Product page | 179 KB | 236 KB |
- **Accessibility:**
  - skip link and `:focus-visible` styles;
  - `aria-expanded`/`aria-pressed`/`aria-current` states;
  - Escape closes the mobile menu;
  - native `<details>` FAQ and `prefers-reduced-motion` support;
  - every `<img>` has an `alt` (0 missing in the generated pages).
  
  A cascade-aware contrast checker, `tools/verifier_contrastes.py`, reports
  « 33 sections analysées sur 15 pages » and « Toutes les sections dépassent
  4.5:1 » (run on 2026-09-27). Proof: `assets/css/*.css`,
  `assets/js/main.js`, commits `0183eb7`, `510cd0c`, `726c01b`.

### Deployment and operations

- **Worker routing:** `/api/*`, `/media/*`, and HTML pages through the Worker

  Proof: `wrangler.toml` (`run_worker_first`), `worker.js`.
- **Deployment exclusions:** `.assetsignore` keeps source code, docs and
  secrets out of the public files. A dry run on 2026-09-25 publishes 246 files
  and nothing sensitive (commit `d1fc37e`).
- **Operator docs:** step-by-step Cloudflare guide, a troubleshooting table
  with messages met in practice, and database migrations with a verification
  script. Proof: `docs/mise-en-ligne-cloudflare.md`,
  `db/migration-001-paiement-hors-ligne.sql`,
  `db/migration-002-catalogue-editable.sql`, `db/verifier-002.sql`.

### Revisions: content integrity, legal, security

- **Fake testimonials removed.** They were replaced by « Personne ne nous a
  encore recommandés » ("Nobody has recommended us yet") and three ways to
  check the atelier yourself. Proof: commit `864b020`, comment in
  `templates/pages/index.html`.
- **Photos with third-party brand marks removed** (a "VT" logo, a stamped
  crest) from the catalogue (2026-08-14) and from the hero video

  `.retirees/POURQUOI.md`, `.retirees/hero-video/POURQUOI.md`, commit

- **The site cannot announce a payment method it does not accept.**
  `controler_paiement()` makes the build fail if card payment is mentioned

- **Unpublishable pieces rejected by the server too**, not only hidden by the
  generator. Proof: `api/_lib/catalog.js` (`priceStandard`), commit
  `d082be6`.
- **Security audit and hardening, 2026-09-25**, with Claude Code. Details are
  in §5; proof: commits `03b0cae`, `0eb4b69`, `d1fc37e`, `b84a317` and
  `docs/securite.md`.

## 4. Key decisions and the assumption behind each one

### 4.1 Order now, pay later

- **Decision.** Checkout takes no payment. The order is saved with status
  `to_confirm`, the atelier is notified on WhatsApp and calls the customer
  back, and payment happens on delivery (cash or Mobile Money) or by transfer.
  Bespoke is the exception: a 50% deposit by transfer, because « le cuir
  s'achète avant de commencer » ("the leather is bought before work starts").
- **Default assumption it breaks.** "An online shop confirms an order by
  taking card payment upfront."
- **Assumptions behind it, as written in the repository:**
  - « Le mode offline existe parce qu'un compte marchand se mérite : pièce
    d'identité, IFU, parfois RCCM, et des semaines d'instruction… la plupart
    des ateliers de la sous-région encaissent à la livraison de toute façon »
    (a merchant account takes ID, a tax number, sometimes company
    registration and weeks of review; most local workshops take payment on
    delivery anyway) (`docs/back-end.md`);
  - a new shop without reviews has to carry the risk itself: « Si nous ne
    tenons pas parole, ça ne vous aura rien coûté » ("if we don't keep our
    word, it will have cost you nothing") (`#confiance`);
  - the business is not registered yet (`mentions-legales.html`).
- **Where it lives:**
  - `wrangler.toml` (`PAYMENT_MODE = "offline"`);
  - `api/_lib/handlers.js` (`onlineAvailable`, `createOrder` payment-mode logic);
  - `db/migration-001-paiement-hors-ligne.sql` (`to_confirm`, `pay_mode`);
  - the checkout « Comment souhaitez-vous régler ? » fieldset (`build_checkout`);
  - `commande-confirmee.html` (message per mode);
  - `.hero__pay`, the marquee « Rien à payer d'avance » and the last FAQ
    entry (`templates/pages/index.html`);
  - `tools/build.py` `controler_paiement()`, which refuses to announce card
    payment while offline;
  - the KkiaPay script is not loaded in offline mode (`build_checkout`
    comment, a privacy reason).
- **What it keeps possible.** The online payment code is complete and switches
  on with configuration alone.

### 4.2 The three guarantees under the CTA

- **Decision.** Right under the only hero button (« Accéder à la boutique »)
  sit three numbers: « 48 h Cotonou & Abidjan », « 1 an d'échange sur la
  sélection », « à vie de ressemelage sur l'atelier ». Just below:
  « Vous commandez sans rien payer… ».
- **Default assumption it breaks.** "Reassurance means reviews, star ratings
  and one blanket 'satisfied or refunded' policy." Here the reassurance is
  concrete, specific to each line, and stands in for reviews the shop does not
  have yet.
- **Assumptions behind it, as written:**
  - « Trois chiffres vrais des deux lignes. « 14 à 20 jours de production » ne
    concernait que l'atelier : sur une page qui vend aussi des pièces
    choisies, il induisait en erreur » ("three numbers true of both lines";
    the old "14 to 20 days of production" only applied to the atelier and was
    misleading) (comment above `.hero__stats`);
  - « Promettre un remboursement intégral sur une pièce achetée à un
    fournisseur qui ne la reprendra pas, c'est engager l'atelier sur une perte
    sèche à chaque retour. Un an d'échange est tenable » ("a full refund on a
    piece the supplier won't take back means a dead loss on every return; a
    one-year exchange is sustainable") (comment in `#garantie`).
- **Where it lives:**
  - `templates/pages/index.html` (`.hero__stats`, `#garantie`, `#confiance`);
  - `data/products.json` (`site.origines.*.garantie`, shown on every category
    page by `methode()`);
  - `cgv.html` §2 and §9, `service-client.html` (« Les deux garanties »);
  - `marque/charte-de-marque.md`, message 1: « Le risque est de notre côté ».

### 4.3 L'atelier (made in-house) vs la sélection (curated)

- **Decision.** Two lines with different guarantees, lead times and wording.
  Every product card and product page carries an origin label, « Fabriqué à
  Cotonou » or « Choisi par l'atelier ». The atelier line, not yet
  photographed, is held back behind « Ce qui se prépare » instead of being
  shown with borrowed images.
- **Default assumption it breaks.** "An artisan brand presents everything it
  sells as its own work" (or a reseller keeps its sourcing quiet).
- **Assumptions behind it, as written:**
  - « Le jour où un client découvre par lui-même qu'une pièce n'est pas de
    l'atelier, la maison perd tout » ("the day a customer finds out on his own
    that a piece isn't from the atelier, the house loses everything");
  - two lines let the house « ajouter des ceintures, des montres, et des
    vêtements dans quelques années, sans se renier » ("add belts, watches and
    clothes in a few years without betraying itself")
    (`marque/charte-de-marque.md`);
  - « huit fiches avec un fond beige et un nom de produit donnent l'impression
    d'une boutique vide » ("eight product pages with a beige background and a
    name make the shop look empty") (`build_a_venir` docstring, "Abou l'a
    demandé ainsi").
- **Where it lives:**
  - `data/products.json` (`site.origines`, `products[].origine`, `a_venir`);
  - `tools/build.py` (`origine_marque`, `methode`, `build_a_venir`);
  - `templates/pages/index.html` (« Les deux lignes », offers, first FAQ entry);
  - `mentions-legales.html` (« Fabriqué ou choisi »), `cgv.html` §2.
- **Effect today, measured in the data.** Every currently orderable product is
  « sélection ». Atelier-made shoes can only be ordered through the bespoke
  configurator, since the atelier shapes are `configurateur_only`.

### 4.4 The « Composer ma paire » configurator

- **Decision.** Bespoke is a 6-step on-screen configurator with an itemised
  price that updates live, and it ends in the cart like any product. The
  atelier then confirms feasibility and date by WhatsApp before any deposit.
  It is reachable from every page header.
- **Default assumption it breaks.** "Made-to-measure means a contact form and
  a quote sent later." The site's own wording: « Pas de devis à attendre, pas
  de « on vous rappelle » » ("no quote to wait for, no 'we'll call you back'").
- **Assumptions behind it, as written:**
  - « Le sur-mesure n'est pas un luxe de vitrine : c'est la réponse normale à
    un pied qui n'entre dans aucune grille » ("bespoke isn't a showroom
    luxury, it's the normal answer to a foot that fits no size chart")
    (`#sur-mesure`);
  - the target customer « a souvent le pied large et aucune pointure ne tombe
    juste » ("often has wide feet and no size fits right") (charter).
- **Where it lives:**
  - `configurateur.html` (generated by `build_configurator`);
  - `assets/js/configurator.js`: the price is recomputed, never accumulated,
    so « un bug d'affichage ne peut donc pas devenir un bug de prix » ("a
    display bug cannot become a pricing bug");
  - `data/products.json` (`site.bespoke`: 4 shapes 158,000–196,000 F, marine
    leather +8,000 F, rubber sole +8,000 F, initials +6,000 F, 50% deposit,
    15 days);
  - `api/_lib/catalog.js` (`priceBespoke`, which re-validates everything on
    the server);
  - `templates/partials/header.html` (« Composer ma paire »);
  - `assets/css/configurator.css` (mobile total bar).

### Other decisions visible in the code

- **One call to action in the hero.** « Deux appels à l'action côte à côte se
  partagent l'attention » ("two CTAs side by side split the attention")
  (comment in `index.html`, commit `d8fc9a7`).
- **No invented reviews** (`864b020`; charter: « Ne pas inventer d'avis
  clients »).
- **AI visuals labelled as such** rather than passed off as product photos
  (footer, `mentions-legales.html`).
- **No analytics and no third-party script beyond Google Fonts**, a trade-off
  the privacy page discloses (`donnees-personnelles.html`).
- **Plain static HTML with no framework**, chosen for mobile connections and
  Google (`api/_lib/rewrite.js` header: « c'est ce qui le rend rapide sur une
  connexion mobile, et ce qui le rend lisible par Google »).

## 5. Technical challenges solved

| Challenge | Solution | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| The 404 page answered `/api/*` routes with HTML | Worker-first routing; 404 served by the Worker | `wrangler.toml` comments, `worker.js` |

| Customer charged but order never confirmed when only the public key is set | Online mode requires all three keys | `api/_lib/handlers.js` (`onlineAvailable`), commit `84c6f59` |
| A double confirmation (page reload plus webhook) could record the same payment twice | `UNIQUE (transaction_id)` plus an idempotent upsert | `db/schema.sql`, `settle()` in `api/_lib/handlers.js` |
| Pieces hidden by the generator could still be ordered with a forged request | Server-side checks in `priceStandard` (`a_venir` added in `d082be6`, alongside the existing `hidden` and `bespoke_only` checks) | `api/_lib/catalog.js` |
| Unreadable sections after the dark-to-light theme switch: the colour tokens passed, the CSS cascade did not | Contrast checker that replays the cascade section by section | `tools/verifier_contrastes.py` docstring; commits `0183eb7`, `4c1132f`, `726c01b`, `510cd0c` |
| Home page grew from 0.7 MB to 7.6 MB with raw PNGs | Export to WebP/JPEG at 500 and 1000 px, aspect ratio kept | `tools/images_ia.py`, commit `18e58a2` |
| Fonts blocking the first render on 3G | `media="print"` stylesheet switched on after load (a script since 2026-09-25) | `templates/base.html` |
| Opening animation that could get stuck | Pure-CSS exit with `forwards`; JS can only shorten it; shown once per session | `templates/base.html`, commits `4d8e4fc`, `55af576` |
| Title measure broken: `ch` computed in Jost but applied to a Playfair heading | Measure set in pixels | comment in `configurateur.html` / `build_configurator` |

| Stale CSS/JS after deploys | Content-hash `?v=` plus immutable caching | `version_assets()` in `tools/build.py`, `_headers` |
