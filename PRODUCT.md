# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Men in Cotonou (Bénin) and Abidjan (Côte d'Ivoire) buying good shoes for the office, ceremonies
and the weekend. They arrive on a phone, most often from a WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook link,
sometimes on a slow or metered connection. They have never handled the piece and have no reviews
to read: they decide from the images, the words and the guarantees, then confirm with the atelier
on WhatsApp. (Confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-20.)

## Product Purpose

Yem's is a house in Cotonou with an atelier at its centre. It sells men's shoes and small leather
goods, ready-made or made to measure, and answers for every piece it sells, whether its own
hands made it or not. Success: a visitor arriving from a social link understands in one screen
what Yem's makes, what it chooses, what each line guarantees, and reaches an order (cart, then a
WhatsApp confirmation) or the bespoke configurator without friction.

## Positioning

Two origins, one house, written above every price: **« Fabriqué à Cotonou »** (the atelier:
hand-stitched point sellier, full-grain vegetable-tanned leather 1.8–2 mm, sole stitched on a
welt and resoleable for life, a number and a card per piece, bespoke in 15 days of atelier work)
and **« Choisi par l'atelier »** (the Sélection: shoes the house did not make, tried, opened and
checked on arrival, one-year exchange, glued soles said plainly to be not resoleable, about half
the price). The honesty about which is which, before the purchase, is the position. **The
atelier leads** the selling pages; the Sélection is the immediate purchase, shown right after.
(Lead line confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-20.)

## Operating Context

- Ordering: add to cart, then a WhatsApp exchange with the atelier confirms the order; nothing is
  paid before that exchange. Payment in cash on delivery or by Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Moov,
  Wave). Online card payment is not active.
- **Trying on at delivery is possible**: the customer can try the pair when it is delivered,
  before paying. (Confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-20.) Which cities it covers and what happens
  if the pair does not fit are not yet specified.
- Bespoke: an on-screen configurator (shape, leather, sole, size 39–46, initials), price shown
  line by line, feasibility and date confirmed by the atelier the same day, 50 % deposit possible.
- Delivery to Bénin and Côte d'Ivoire; the WhatsApp of the atelier answers Monday to Saturday,
  8 h – 19 h.
- Local life the copy already uses: the ceremony Saturday, the 14 h heat, the wet June asphalt.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static French site generated by `python tools/build.py` from `templates/`, `templates/partials/`
  and `data/products.json`; vanilla CSS and JS, no framework, no animation library (none to be
  added). Generated HTML is never edited by hand.
- A Cloudflare Worker rewrites prices, badges and add-to-cart buttons at the edge
  (`data-prix`, `data-court`, `data-badge`, `data-add-to-cart` attributes must survive).
- Cart in `localStorage` (`yems.cart`).
- Catalogue: 15 pieces in five categories (Chaussures, Ceintures, Portefeuilles, Entretien —
  atelier; Sélection — chosen). Only the seven Sélection pieces have a product page today; atelier
  pieces route to « à venir » or to the configurator.
- This redesign covers the selling pages first: home, shop (`boutique`), category pages, product
  pages. Configurator, cart, checkout, legal pages inherit the shared styles only, to be redone
  once the style is validated.
- Undecided / not yet supplied: final prices (all current figures indicative, « À valider »
  stays visible), delivery fees and confirmed delivery times, atelier address, contact e-mail,
  legal identity, the « pack complet ».

## Brand Commitments

- Name **Yem's**, signature **« Différents par les détails »**, domain maisonyems.com.
- Visual register: the category standard of premium shoe houses, executed at full craft, chosen
  by the owner over two bolder directions (2026-11-20). Quality bar: Corthay, Crockett & Jones,
  Septième Largeur.
- Voice: short, concrete, first person plural, honest about limits; French; « vous » to the
  customer.
- Every price, origin, status, guarantee and legal text stays faithful to `data/products.json`
  and the CGV.
- Every product visual is AI-generated and must stay disclosed as such (footer line and image
  mentions); never presented as a photograph of the delivered piece.

## Evidence on Hand

- 27 AI-generated visuals in `assets/img/` (studio portraits on a warm cognac backdrop, atelier
  hands stitching, products on a travertine plinth, leather swatches, one city street scene).
  New AI visuals will be generated by the owner with ChatGPT from prompts supplied in the chat.
- The full current copy of every page (`templates/pages/*.html`) and the catalogue data.
- Absent, and never to be fabricated: customer reviews or ratings, sales or customer counts,
  years in business, artisan names, press, real photographs, atelier address.

## Product Principles

1. Say where each piece comes from before saying what it costs.
2. The atelier is the reason the house exists; the Sélection is the honest complement.
3. Promise only what the house can keep, in writing, before the purchase.
4. Built for a phone on a weak connection first: light pages, thumb-reachable actions.
5. Nothing invented: what is unknown stays generic and visibly provisional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA as the floor: readable contrast on every ground, visible focus, keyboard paths,
`prefers-reduced-motion` honoured, image dimensions reserved, touch targets of 44 px.
